The Panasonic camera reports 2026-03-28. The Samsung reports 2013-10-16, which is inconsistent with the Green Line Extension pictured and cannot date the trip. Neither EXIF value is presented as a verified trip date. Clock values have no trustworthy time zone; do not merge or display them as a shared timeline. The cameras overlap on the Red return.

## Relative timing

No backward EXIF jumps occur within the supplied filename sequences. The long gaps could reflect inactive cameras, battery failures, or clock changes; they cannot distinguish those causes. The rest of each continuous station chunk has increasing times and plausible travel gaps, so relative timing supports local sequence assignments without overriding readable signs.

- Panasonic P1010993 → P1010994: 262-minute gap between a Red train interior and the Park Street finish portrait; the other camera covers much of that interval.
- Samsung SAM_0644 → SAM_0645: 154-minute gap; SAM_0645 → SAM_0646: 324-minute gap. Treat these early fragments separately from the later continuous Samsung sequence.
- Samsung SAM_0645 appears to be a Green B platform (Babcock/Amory), rather than a Red station. Its camera time is roughly 28–30 minutes before the Panasonic B branch shots, consistent with a possible offset but insufficient to certify either station.

There is no invented global clock correction, trip date, or total time. Relative camera times are visible on the local review page only.

Relative timing resolved two more frames: P1010797 is most likely Saint Paul Street (42 seconds before confirmed Coolidge Corner), and P1010811 is most likely Fairbanks Street (between confirmed Washington Square and Brandon Hall, the only intermediate stop). Both remain labeled inferred on the public page. P1010796 is therefore more likely Kent than Hawes, but it remains under review. P1010802 is only 16 seconds before a Washington Square sign, so the earlier Brandon Hall guess is weakened; Fairbanks vicinity or in-transit scenery remain possible.
